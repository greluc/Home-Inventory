**Status:** ongoing · **Date:** 2026-09-11

A collection point for decisions not yet taken.

> **As of 2026-09-11: no open decision remains.** The architecture review of that
> day closed eight questions and surfaced four more (O11–O14), all decided. A
> second review pass the same day surfaced six defects and produced
> [ADR-0036](0036-scanner-egress.md) to
> [ADR-0041](0041-migration-as-its-own-service.md), including **O15** (raised and
> closed the same day) and two calls the review had made on its own and put back
> to the repository owner — where the migration credential lives, and where the
> appearance preference lives. The tables below are the record of what was
> decided, when and how. New open points are added here.
>
> **A third review pass the same day** re-read the whole corpus. It found
> twenty-odd places where an earlier correction had been applied
> in three documents and missed in a fourth — a withdrawn `COEP` header, a
> `Warning` header dropped in one paragraph and reinstated nine lines later, the
> pre-[ADR-0032](0032-per-tenant-blob-addressing.md) blob path, and the
> [ADR-0042](0042-edge-is-not-internal.md) claim still standing in
> [`deploy/services.yaml`](../../deploy/services.yaml) four lines above the
> measurement that refutes it. It also found the defect that was not a wording
> problem: the default media store existed in no topology at all, which produced
> [ADR-0043](0043-blobstore-as-its-own-service.md). It raised five further
> decisions — **O18** to **O22** — and all five were decided the same day and are
> in the table below.
>
> The pattern is risk **R16**, and it recurred because the `Amends:` header of an
> ADR is hand-maintained: [ADR-0040](0040-no-cross-origin-isolation.md) named four
> of the six places it reached, and `COEP` survived in the other two. **A4 below**
> is the countermeasure.
