> ADR is hand-maintained: [ADR-0040](0040-no-cross-origin-isolation.md) named four
> of the six places it reached, and `COEP` survived in the other two. **A4 below**
> is the countermeasure.
>
> **A fourth review pass, the same day**, checked the corpus against the things it can be
> checked against rather than against itself: the token values, the arithmetic, the
> vendors' documentation, and the delivered design system. It produced four ADRs and
> found the class of defect the three earlier passes could not, because all three were
> reading.
>
> The largest was a **security gap, not a drift**: `internal` was a flat segment where
> only PostgreSQL held a credential, and `web` — the one container with a published port —
> was a full member of it ([ADR-0044](0044-internal-is-not-a-trust-boundary.md)). RabbitMQ
> had no credential at all, which also meant the generated stack could not have started.
> Beside it: **`REQ-NFR-014` promised an RPO of 15 minutes and nothing archived WAL
> anywhere** ([ADR-0045](0045-wal-archive-volume.md)); **`REQ-PRIV-010`'s per-tenant audit
> retention could not be carried out** without triggering ADR-0031's own tampering signal
> ([ADR-0046](0046-truncatable-audit-chain.md)); and the stage-0 full-text index used the
> one PostgreSQL configuration that does no stemming
> ([ADR-0047](0047-bilingual-search-vectors.md)).
>
> Three further findings needed no ADR because they were plain error. `BarcodeDetector`
> does not exist in WebKit, so [10 §10.3](../architecture/10-identification-and-labels.md)'s
> *"Safari 17+"* was wrong and ZXing-WASM is the **only** browser scanning path on iOS —
> risk **R17**. Five of the 64 recorded contrast ratios in `tokens.json` did not
> reproduce, one of them (20.4) above the theoretical maximum of 21 for its colour, so
> `REQ-NFR-077`'s CI check would have failed on its first run. And fourteen design-system
> components carried hard-coded German display text against `REQ-NFR-032`, while every
